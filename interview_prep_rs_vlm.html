RS-VLM Master Interview Guide

A comprehensive preparation kit with 25+ specialized questions covering Model Architecture, ML Engineering, and Remote Sensing domain expertise.

1. Architecture & Design Choices

1 Why a Hybrid CNN-ViT instead of a standard Vision Transformer?
Surface

CNNs are "local experts" (seeing roads, cars), while ViTs are "global thinkers" (seeing forests, cities). Satellite images need both scales to be understood accurately.

Deep Dive

ViTs lack inductive bias. In Remote Sensing, objects like small buildings are just a few pixels. A pure ViT patch (16x16) might swallow multiple objects into one token. The CNN Stem (ResNet-18) preserves high-resolution spatial hierarchies, which are then passed to the ViT Body for long-range dependency modeling via self-attention.

2 Explain the "Log-Normalization" in your GSD Adapter.
Deep Dive

Ground Sample Distance (GSD) varies exponentially (0.3m for WorldView to 60m for Landsat). A linear scale would cause gradients to explode or vanish for extreme values. I used torch.log(gsd.clamp(min=1e-3)) to compress the range into a manageable feature space for the MLP, ensuring the positional bias is stable regardless of sensor resolution.

3 Why TinyLlama-1.1B over a 7B or 13B model?
Surface

It balances power and efficiency. 1.1B parameters is plenty for land-use classification chat, but it fits on a single consumer GPU.

Deep Dive

Scaling to 7B increases VRAM usage by ~7x. For an undergraduate project, the priority is iteration speed. TinyLlama allows for faster experimental cycles in Phase 2 and 3 while still using the modern Llama-2 architecture (RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU), making the skills learned directly transferable to larger models.

4 What happens if you remove the MLP Projector?
Deep Dive

The code would crash immediately due to a dimension mismatch (384 vs 2048). But theoretically, even if the dimensions matched, the projector is needed for modality alignment. It transforms visual latent features into the specific embedding manifold that the LLM's language-pretrained layers expect.

5 Why use Positional Bias instead of a scaling layer for GSD?
Deep Dive

GSD changes how we interpret distance between pixels. By adding the GSD-conditioned vector to the ViT's positional embeddings, we effectively "re-calibrate" the model's sense of spatial distance. A scaling layer would only change the intensity of features, not their spatial context.

2. Training Pipeline & Optimization

6 Explain your 3-Phase training strategy.
Surface

See first, Translate second, Talk third. Each phase builds on the last to prevent the model from getting confused.

Deep Dive

Phase 1 (MAE): Self-supervised pretraining on EuroSAT to learn visual features.
Phase 2 (Alignment): Trains *only* the Projector on RSICD image-caption pairs to bridge the vision-language gap.
Phase 3 (Instruction Tuning): Fine-tunes the LLM using LoRA on VQA templates to enable conversational interaction.

7 What is "Catastrophic Forgetting" and how did you prevent it?
Deep Dive

If we fine-tuned the whole 1.1B model on EuroSAT, it would forget its English grammar and general knowledge. I prevented this by freezing the LLM backbone and using LoRA (Phase 3). Only 1% of the weights are updated, preserving the original intelligence of the model while adding satellite-specific skills.

8 How did you handle 75% masking in MAE?
Deep Dive

I used mae_mask_patches in Phase 1. Masking 75% is standard for MAE because images have high redundancy. It forces the encoder to learn "dense" semantic representations to reconstruct the missing pixels from a very sparse set of visible ones.

9 What is Gradient Checkpointing?
Surface

A memory-saving trick that trades speed for space.

Deep Dive

Normally, all intermediate activations are stored for the backward pass. In Phase 2/3, this causes OOM. Checkpointing drops activations and recomputes them during the backward pass. This allows training 1B+ models on 12GB-16GB GPUs.

10 Why use AdamW instead of standard Adam?
Deep Dive

AdamW correctly implements Weight Decay by decoupling it from the gradient update. This leads to better generalization, especially in transformers where over-parameterization can easily lead to overfitting.

3. Remote Sensing & Data Engineering

11 What is the "Zero-Up" problem in satellite imagery?
Surface

There is no "up" in space. Photos can be taken from any angle.

Deep Dive

Satellite sensors have no fixed orientation relative to the objects they image. To make my model Rotation Invariant, I applied heavy data augmentation (RandomRotation 90, 180, 270) to ensure the vision tokens remain semantic regardless of the image orientation.

12 Why is RGB sometimes "not enough" for satellite AI?
Deep Dive

Satellites like Sentinel-2 have 13 bands (Near-Infrared, Red-Edge, etc.). These bands show things the human eye can't see, like vegetation health (NDVI) or moisture levels. My architecture is modular to allow adding these extra channels in the CNN stem in the future.

13 How do you handle cloud cover?
Surface

Clouds hide the data. We either filter them out or teach the model to ignore them.

Deep Dive

In Phase 1 MAE, the model naturally learns to "reconstruct" missing data. This makes it inherently more robust to small cloud occlusions compared to a model that has only ever seen perfect ImageNet photos.

14 Why use the EuroSAT dataset?
Surface

It's the industry standard "benchmark" for land-use classification.

Deep Dive

EuroSAT contains 27,000 multi-spectral images across 10 classes. It's clean, labeled, and diverse enough (covering all of Europe) to serve as a high-quality foundation for Phase 1 and Phase 3 tuning.

15 How does RSICD differ from EuroSAT?
Deep Dive

EuroSAT is a Classification dataset (Image -> Label). RSICD is a Captioning dataset (Image -> Sentence). We need both: EuroSAT for visual features and RSICD for language alignment.

4. ML Engineering & Production

16 How did you implement Loss Masking in Phase 3?
Deep Dive

I calculated the length of the user's prompt tokens and set their labels to -100. Since CrossEntropyLoss ignores -100, the model is only penalized for predicting the Assistant's answer tokens incorrectly. This is key for instruction tuning.

17 What is a GradScaler?
Deep Dive

In FP16 training, gradients can become so small they turn into zero (Underflow). The GradScaler multiplies the loss by a factor (e.g., 65536) to keep the numbers large during backprop, then shrinks them back down before the optimizer step.

18 Why use Streamlit for the UI?
Surface

It's the fastest way to turn a Python script into a beautiful web app.

Deep Dive

Streamlit allows me to keep the entire project in Pure Python. It handles image uploads and session state automatically, allowing me to focus on the chat_with_image logic instead of writing JavaScript or HTML/CSS.

19 If the model is slow, how would you optimize inference?
Deep Dive

I would use Quantization (4-bit or 8-bit) using bitsandbytes, or convert the model to ONNX/TensorRT. I could also implement KV-Caching to speed up the autoregressive text generation.

20 How do you debug a "NaN" loss during training?
Deep Dive

1. Check if the LR is too high. 2. Look for divisions by zero in custom layers (like GSD log-norm). 3. Use torch.autograd.set_detect_anomaly(True) to find the exact line causing the crash. 4. Ensure GradScaler is working correctly.

5. Behavioral & Critical Thinking

21 What was the biggest challenge in this project?

"Managing the hardware constraints. Fitting a Vision Encoder and an LLM into memory while maintaining a reasonable batch size required careful use of Gradient Checkpointing and LoRA. It taught me the importance of memory-efficient AI."

22 How would you handle a "Multi-Label" image (e.g., a city with a forest)?
Deep Dive

Currently, the model might only pick one. To fix this, I would need to move from EuroSAT (single labels) to a more complex dataset like SkyQA or RSICD for Phase 3 tuning, using multi-part answers in the templates.

23 "Your model is 1.1B parameters. Is that too big for a satellite?"
Surface

For a satellite's onboard computer? Yes. For a ground station? No.

Deep Dive

RS-VLM is designed for Ground-Side Analysis (Cloud/Server). For Edge-AI on a satellite, I would need to use a much smaller backbone like MobileNetV3 and a quantized DistilBERT or a 100M-parameter custom LLM.

24 How do you know the model isn't just memorizing the captions?
Deep Dive

By using a **Validation Set** that the model has never seen during training. If the validation loss follows the training loss without diverging, we know the model is generalizing. Also, checking the Qualitative Output on random images from the web proves it understands the concepts.

25 What is the next step for RS-VLM?

"Moving beyond 224x224 resolution. Many satellite features are tiny. Increasing input resolution to 512x512 would allow the model to detect smaller objects like individual cars or shipping containers, making it even more useful for real-world monitoring."
